Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

At 1337Ops ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from 1337ops.tech, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and clients. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by 1337Ops and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at payton@1337ops.tech.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users and clients. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, company name, and phone number when you fill out our contact form or communicate directly with us.
  • Engagement & Project Data: Technical specifications, infrastructure details, credentials, and requirements shared during consulting engagements, security assessments, and AI automation projects.
  • Log Files & Analytics: Standard web analytics information such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and consulting services.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our offerings, cloud architectures, and AI workflows.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website and services.
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through one of our partners, for customer service, updates, and technical discussions.
  • Send you project proposals, invoices, technical reports, and security assessment findings.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, security breaches, or unauthorized access.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

1337Ops is founded by security professionals (Certified Ethical Hackers and AWS Certified Solutions Architects). We treat client data, infrastructure secrets, and intellectual property with the highest standard of confidentiality:

  • All sensitive credentials, API keys, and access tokens shared during engagements are handled securely and purged upon project completion in accordance with client agreements.
  • Communications are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or proprietary business data to third parties.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, 1337Ops uses cookies to store visitor preferences and to optimize the user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Our Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites (such as GitHub, LinkedIn, or external cloud providers). We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. 1337Ops does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
